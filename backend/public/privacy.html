Privacy Policy

What We Collect

  • Page URLs and extracted text content (sent for AI analysis when you click Analyze)
  • Anonymous device identifier (a random UUID generated on install)
  • Daily usage counts per device (to enforce the free analysis limit)

What We Don't Collect

  • Personal information (name, email, address)
  • Browsing history beyond pages you choose to analyze
  • Passwords, form data, or payment information
  • Data from pages you don't analyze

How Data Is Used

  • Page content is sent to the OpenAI API for AI-powered fact-check analysis
  • Analysis results are cached for 24 hours to reduce API costs and improve speed
  • Shared fact-checks are stored permanently (only when you click "Share")
  • Device usage counts enforce the daily analysis limit (10 per day)

Third Parties

We use OpenAI as our AI analysis provider. Page content you analyze is sent to OpenAI's API for processing. OpenAI's privacy policy applies to their handling of this data.

Data Retention

  • Analysis cache: 24 hours (automatically deleted)
  • Shared fact-checks: permanent (user-initiated only)
  • Device usage logs: 30 days (automatically deleted)

Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact us at privacy@debunked.live.
